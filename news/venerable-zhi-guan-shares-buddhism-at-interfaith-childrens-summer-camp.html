Venerable Zhi Guan Shares Buddhism at Interfaith Children's Summer Camp

July 7, 2026

Interfaith

At the invitation of Venerable Ananda of Lake Monastery in Cambridge, Ontario, Venerable Zhi Guan spent July 7 introducing Buddhism to around 50 children attending a multi-faith summer camp's “Exploring World Religions Day.”

He built the session around two central symbols — the Dharma wheel and the Eightfold Path — using them to walk the children through the basics of Buddhist practice in terms a young audience could follow. The day's broader program also brought the campers through several other houses of worship, including a mosque, a Sikh gurdwara, and a church, so they could compare traditions directly rather than simply hear about them.
