Toronto Buddha's Light Choir Opens Multicultural Choir Festival

July 4, 2026

Music

The Toronto Buddha's Light Choir, under conductor Monie Liang, opened the 35th Overseas Chinese Cultural Arts Season choir showcase on July 4 by leading the Canadian national anthem at Mississauga's Living Arts Centre.

Fourteen Chinese choirs and more than 520 performers took part in the citywide event, giving Toronto's choral community a chance to perform for one another over a single afternoon. Beyond the anthem, the temple's choir also presented a set of humanistic Buddhist songs, continuing its practice of using performance opportunities like this one to introduce Buddhist teachings to a wider, largely secular audience.
