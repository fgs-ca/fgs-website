Supreme Patriarch of Bangladesh Buddhist Sangha Visits Fo Guang Shan Toronto

July 6, 2026

Visit

On the evening of July 6, His Holiness Dr. Banasree Mahathero, the 87-year-old Supreme Patriarch of the Bangladesh Buddhist Sangha Council, visited Fo Guang Shan Temple of Toronto accompanied by Bhante Saranapala of Toronto's West End Buddhist Temple, along with a monastic attendant and several lay members.

Venerable Zhi Guan and local outreach coordinator Qiu Weishan welcomed the delegation, leading them in prayers at the temple's main shrine hall and giving an overview of Fo Guang Shan's presence in more than 200 temples worldwide. The two sides exchanged ceremonial gifts to mark the visit, a gesture reflecting the growing exchange between the temple and other Buddhist communities across the Greater Toronto Area.
