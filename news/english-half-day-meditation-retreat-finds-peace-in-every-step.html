English Half-Day Meditation Retreat Finds Peace in Every Step

July 4, 2026

Practice

Eleven participants — two of whom drove in from Ottawa — spent July 4 at an English-language half-day meditation retreat themed “Find Peace in Every Step.”

The program moved between seated meditation, walking meditation both indoors and outdoors, and a mindful-eating session, interspersed with short Dharma sharing to connect the practices back to daily life. Participants left with laser-engraved, eco-friendly lunch boxes and a Buddhist text as keepsakes, meant as small reminders to carry the day's calm back into their routines.
