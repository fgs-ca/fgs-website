Fo Guang University Students Complete Summer Internship at Fo Guang Shan Toronto

July 25, 2026

Education

Six students from Fo Guang University in Taiwan — including Chen Bo-wei from Information Application, Wang Yu-tong from Communications, Chien Cheng-yu from Media Production, and Huang Shun-wei from Social Work — spent this summer on a two-month internship at the temple, led by faculty advisor Professor Huang Chiu-lien.

The group rotated through several departments, including the temple library, the spiritual care team, the IT and communications office, and its community education classes, applying what they had studied to real day-to-day operations. About a month into the placement, the students gathered to share early reflections and thank the staff who had been mentoring them. By the internship's end, organizers said the experience had broadened the students' professional skills as well as their international outlook.
