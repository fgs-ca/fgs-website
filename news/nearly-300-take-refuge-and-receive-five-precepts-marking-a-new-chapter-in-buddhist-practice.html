Nearly 300 Take Refuge and Receive Five Precepts, Marking a New Chapter in Buddhist Practice

August 8, 2026

Ceremony

On August 8, nearly 300 people ranging in age from a three-month-old infant to a 93-year-old took refuge in the Triple Gem and received the Five Precepts in a ceremony presided over by Venerable Hui Kai, Deputy Abbot of Fo Guang Shan, with the rehearsal led beforehand by Venerable Yung Han, Abbess of Fo Guang Shan Chicago.

Participants travelled from Montreal, Chicago, and Detroit as well as from across Canada and the United States — among them Maria An of the International Buddhist Progress Society of Montreal, who drove six hours to attend, and four Fo Guang University interns, including Wesley Wang and Iris Chien, who also took refuge and received the precepts that day. Monika Oroszlan, a member of the temple's Lotus Mornings meditation group for the past year, received the Dharma name “Ren Ke” as part of the ceremony. The occasion doubled as a milestone celebration, marking both the temple's 30th anniversary in Toronto and Fo Guang Shan's 60th anniversary worldwide.
