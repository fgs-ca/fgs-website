Giving as Practice

In the Buddhist tradition, the making of an offering allows one to practice giving, to express gratitude and respect, and to reflect upon the life-sustaining law of interdependence.

How to Give

Four Ways to Donate

Cash

Paid directly at the temple during office hours.

Cheque

Made payable to I.B.P.S. of Toronto, mailed to 6525 Millcreek Drive, Mississauga, ON L5N 7K6.

Interac e-Transfer

Send to donation@fgs.ca — please include the purpose of your donation in the memo.

PayPal

Via the PayPal Giving Fund — no processing fees, with tax receipts issued directly by PayPal.

Note: for donation forms, the temple recommends using a laptop or desktop computer with the Chrome browser for the best experience.

Donation Categories

  • General donations
  • Sangha offerings
  • Dharma protector memberships
  • Ceremony offerings (Great Compassion Repentance, Emperor Liang rituals)
  • Light & lantern blessings
  • Memorial & blessing plaques
  • University research institute support
  • Children’s novice programs
Questions?

We’re Happy to Help

Reach out to the temple office if you have questions about donation categories, tax receipts, or ceremony offerings.
