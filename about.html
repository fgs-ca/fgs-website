About the Temple

A Spiritual and Cultural Home

Fo Guang Shan Temple of Toronto has served as an educational, cultural, and spiritual center for the Greater Toronto Area since 1991.

Our Mission

Humanistic Buddhism

The temple propagates Humanistic Buddhism, which emphasizes bringing Buddhist teaching into our daily lives and maintaining harmony with the environment, society, each other, and within ourselves.

As a spiritual, cultural, and educational center, we welcome anyone curious about Buddhism and Chinese culture — regardless of background.

Four Core Objectives

  • Propagate Buddhist teachings through cultural activities
  • Nurture talents through education
  • Benefit societies through charitable programs
  • Purify human hearts and minds through Buddhist practice
Our History

From Groundbreaking to Consecration

1991

Founded

Established by Venerable Master Hsing Yun after Toronto devotees requested a temple during his visit. Initially organized as I.B.P.S. Toronto.

1994

Groundbreaking

Construction began on the temple's Mississauga site.

1997

Consecration

The temple was completed and officially consecrated on August 10, 1997.

Today

I.B.P.S. of Toronto

Now known as Fo Guang Shan Temple of Toronto, serving the community with satellite centers in Markham and Kitchener-Waterloo.

Our Facility

50,000 Square Feet in Mississauga

Our main temple houses a magnificent main shrine, tea house, traditional Chan meditation hall, Buddhist cultural museum, library, and bookstore.

The Fo Guang Shan Library of Toronto holds approximately 20,000 items in multiple languages.

On-Site Facilities

  • Main shrine hall
  • Water Drop Teahouse
  • Traditional Chan meditation hall
  • Buddhist cultural museum
  • Library & bookstore
Part of a Global Network

Affiliated with ~300 Temples Worldwide

Fo Guang Shan Temple of Toronto is affiliated with approximately 300 Fo Guang Shan temples worldwide, connected to the international Buddhist organization founded in Taiwan in 1967.
